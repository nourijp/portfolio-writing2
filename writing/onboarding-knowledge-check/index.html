A Knowledge Check That Explains the Answer

A short training assessment with feedback for each decision.

Audience New users learning a fictional dashboard application

Adapted portfolio sample using a fictional product and scenario.

This check follows the dashboard import guide. Use it to review the decisions involved in selecting and saving a template.

Question 1

You want to start from a dashboard your team has already prepared. Where should you begin?

  • A. Create an empty dashboard.
  • B. Open the template library.
  • C. Change your profile settings.

Answer B. The template library contains shared starting points. Creating an empty dashboard would mean selecting the contents yourself.

Question 2

A template includes several panels you will not use. What should you do before saving your personal copy?

  • A. Remove the panels you do not need.
  • B. Ask everyone on the team to change their dashboards.
  • C. Save several identical copies.

Answer A. Review the imported template and adjust it for your task. The guide describes creating a personal copy.

Question 3

How do you confirm that the task is complete?

  • A. Close the browser as soon as the preview appears.
  • B. Check only the template description.
  • C. Save the dashboard, then open it from your dashboard list.

Answer C. Opening the saved dashboard confirms that your selection is available for use.

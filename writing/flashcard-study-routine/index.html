Educational WritingStudy Guide

A Simple Flashcard Study Routine

Learner-facing instructions built around recall, feedback, and review.

Audience People beginning to use digital flashcards

Adapted portfolio sample using a fictional product and scenario.

Use a flashcard session to check what you can recall before you look at the answer.

Work through a card

  1. Choose the deck you want to study.
  2. Start the review session.
  3. Read the question or prompt.
  4. Try to say or write the answer before revealing it.
  5. Reveal the answer and compare it with your response.
  6. Rate how difficult the card was to recall.

Your rating helps the application determine when to show the card again. The exact interval depends on the application and your review history.

Use the rating honestly

If you recognized the answer only after revealing it, record that the card needs more practice. Recognition after seeing an answer is different from recalling it yourself.

Notice what causes difficulty

If a card repeatedly causes confusion, check the wording. A prompt that asks several questions may be easier to study when divided into smaller cards.

Finish the session by noting one topic you want to revisit.
